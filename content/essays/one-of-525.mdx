Limited editions usually pick their numbers for the marketing department — a founding year, a
round thousand. Breitling's new Top Time picks its number from the record book. The Eddy Merckx
edition runs to exactly 525 pieces, one for every race the man won, and that single decision
tells you the people behind it did their reading. Merckx's 525 victories are not a fan's
rounding; they are the most extraordinary tally in the sport's history — eleven Grand Tours,
nineteen Monuments, five Tours de France, and the [Hour Record](/features/the-longest-hour) he
took in Mexico City in 1972 and called the hardest ride of his life. The edition count is the
palmarès, wearable.

The rest of the watch keeps that level of specificity. The dial is Tour de France yellow — not
approximately, but deliberately: the maillot jaune is the jersey Merckx wore for a record 96
days, longer than anyone before or since. His signature sits above six o'clock where a
sub-brand line would normally go, and his name runs in red along the tachymeter scale. The
caseback carries the plain sentence that gives this piece its title: *Eddy Merckx Tribute — One
of 525*. Underneath it, visible through sapphire, runs Breitling's B01 — the company's own
column-wheel, vertical-clutch chronograph calibre, [COSC-certified](/guides/what-a-chronometer-actually-is)
and good for roughly seventy hours off the wrist. A 41mm steel case, 100 metres of water
resistance, $8,050 on perforated black calfskin or $8,450 on steel.

## The shelf it joins

The Merckx is the third rider in the line, and the company built the set in the right order.
The Top Time cycling editions began with Fausto Coppi and Gino Bartali — 750 pieces each, and a
pairing no student of the sport could improve on, because Coppi against Bartali was the rivalry
that split post-war Italy in two. The Coppi wears a white dial with turquoise subdials and an
orange lightning-bolt seconds hand; the Bartali wears the blue of its namesake's jersey, and
carries the heavier story — the wartime years in which Bartali used training rides to courier
false documents for hidden Jewish families, work that later saw him honoured as Righteous Among
the Nations. Then, above them both, the Cannibal, in yellow, in a smaller edition, because 525
is not a number you inflate.

## Why this one rings true

This site keeps a simple test for sport-themed watches: are the references the kind one rider
would make to another, or the kind a brand makes at a trade fair? [Bravur passed that
test](/features/the-last-kilometre) with a flamme rouge index and an upside-down 13. The Top
Time editions pass it in a different register — not insider superstition but archive work.
Victory counts as edition sizes. Jersey colours as dials. A rivalry presented as a pair, and
the man who out-won them all presented alone. Nothing here needed a caption, and nothing got
one.

It also completes a picture of Breitling's unusual position in this sport. One arm of the
company builds the [Endurance Pro](/guides/the-breitling-endurance-pro), a featherweight
quartz tool the Triathlon Squad actually races in. This arm builds mechanical tributes to the
riders who made the sport worth commemorating — heritage at the price of heritage. Between the
two sits an honest division: the watch for doing the thing, and the watch for having loved the
people who did it best. A brand that understands the difference, and builds properly for both,
is rarer in cycling than it should be.
