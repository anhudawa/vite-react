# Social launch kit — The Long Second

Launch posts for the ten flagship essays, plus three evergreen brand posts.
Voice per `content/voice/VOICE.md`. Every factual claim is drawn from the
essay's dek or tldr — no new claims. Image status per `docs/IMAGE-GAPS.md`
(refreshed 2026-07-03).

Conventions:

- X posts are under 280 characters including the URL (X counts any URL as 23).
  One hashtag maximum; most posts carry none.
- Instagram links go in bio (or story link); captions end with "Essay via link
  in bio." Each caption carries the image status so scheduling follows rights.
- LinkedIn posts run 3–4 sentences, a touch more explanatory, same voice.
- Base URL: https://thelongsecond.com
