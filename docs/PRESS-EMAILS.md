# Press-kit request emails — Batch 3

Drafts for the six Batch-3 imagery asks in `docs/IMAGE-GAPS.md`, ready for
Anthony to send from his own account. Four emails cover five of the six asks —
the chronograph-guide ask ("any brand chronograph press frame, pushers
prominent") is folded into the Bravur email, since the Grand Tour Sprinter is
a mechanical chronograph and the guide already points readers at that piece.
The sixth ask (`the-instrument-of-effort`) is flagged in IMAGE-GAPS as
possibly imageless by design; no email needed unless that decision changes.

Ground rules baked into every draft (per `data/brand.ts` — contact email,
founding year and base location are still unfilled):

- One honest sentence about the site. No traffic numbers, no founding year,
  no location, no claimed publication contact address.
- Exactly what is requested, and nothing offered that we can't deliver — no
  promised review coverage, no guaranteed placement beyond the named pieces.
- Credit and licence handling stated plainly: full credit as the brand
  specifies it; licence terms recorded per file (they land in the essay's
  `image.credit` / `image.rights` meta and `data/rights.ts`).
- Site register: plain, warm, dry, no exclamation marks, none of the
  EDITORIAL.md banned words.
- `{{URL}}` / `{{URL-…}}` = the live essay link at send time.
  `{{signature}}` = Anthony's own sign-off (name, site, and whatever contact
  details he chooses to give — none are hard-coded here because none are
  canonical yet).
